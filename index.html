The meaning of Ekalai

Learning through observation & practice

Ekalai began with a simple belief - art isn’t something you’re either born with or without. It’s something you grow into: by looking closely, practising often and trusting your own ideas.

The name carries that spirit into every class we teach - for a six-year-old holding a pencil for the first time, and for an adult rediscovering the joy of drawing.

Curiosity

Asking “what if?” and looking at the world a little more closely.

Self-Learning

Finding the confidence to explore, try and figure things out.

Consistent Practice

Small, regular steps that slowly turn into real skill.

Growth Through Observation

Learning to see, before learning to draw.

Our teaching philosophy

Your style first. Everything else follows.

Most art classes begin with copying - the same picture, the same steps, and the goal of making it look exactly real. There’s a place for that. But when it comes first, many learners lose the very thing that makes their art theirs. At Ekalai, we start the other way around. We help every student discover their own lines, characters and stories - and once that creative foundation is strong, we guide them into realism and advanced techniques as the natural next step.

The usual way

  • Copy a reference exactly
  • One “right” way to draw
  • Hyper-realism from day one
  • Same pace for everyone
  • Fear of making mistakes

The Ekalai way

  • Begin with your own ideas
  • Many ways to express yourself
  • Creativity first; realism grows with experience
  • Personal attention for each learner
  • Mistakes are part of creating

Abishek Rajendran Founder & Mentor, Ekalai

For more than 12 years, Abishek has been exploring art, illustration, animation and visual storytelling - through learning, practice and real-world projects.

His own journey didn’t follow a fixed path. He learned by studying great artists, learning from industry professionals, practising every day and working on real projects.
